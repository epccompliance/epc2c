Why this matters

1 Oct 2030
All privately rented homes in England & Wales must reach EPC C by this date.
£10,000
The cost cap per property. Spend up to this on improvements; if C still isn't reached, an exemption may apply.
Up to £30,000
The maximum penalty for letting a non-compliant property once the rules are in force.
Already done some of this work?
If you've installed measures since your last EPC, your true rating may already be higher. A fresh EPC (£99) confirms it — and may show you're closer to C, or already there.
Grants & funding. † Warm Homes Plan funding is not yet targeted at the private rented sector, but is expected to extend to it — eligibility will be updated as the scheme develops. The Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant applies to qualifying heat-pump installations. 0% finance availability depends on the provider and your circumstances.
