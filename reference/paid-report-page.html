The most cost-effective route to C

The cheapest combination of measures that takes this property to band C. Costs are indicative ranges from the EPC register — always get installer quotes before committing.

Top up loft insulation to 270mm
Indicative cost£100–£350
Rating gain+3 pts
DisruptionLow
Warm Homes Plan †0% finance available
Feasibility — worth checking first
  • A usable loft hatch (ideally ~600×400mm) and enough headroom to work safely.
  • If the loft is boarded, boards may need lifting — or raising on battens — so insulation depth isn’t crushed.
  • Stored items cleared from the area to be treated.
If your EPC marked this “assumed”: Loft insulation can’t be seen without entering the loft, so "assumed" usually means it wasn’t accessed or confirmed. Two implications: it may already be insulated (a fresh EPC could show you’re closer to C), or access is the very thing that makes the job harder.

Feasibility — worth checking first
  • A suitable roof: reasonable orientation (broadly south/east/west, not heavily shaded north), workable pitch, minimal overshadowing.
  • Roof structure sound and able to carry the load.
  • Enough clear, unobstructed roof area, plus a sensible position for the inverter.
If your EPC marked this “assumed”: Generation is recorded as present/absent rather than "assumed", so the question here is roof feasibility rather than an unverified baseline.
