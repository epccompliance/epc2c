Not an official EPC

EPC2C is not an official Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) and does not constitute a domestic energy assessment.

The compliance planning tool uses data from the government EPC register and GoGreen Alliance’s retrofit experience to produce planning-level estimates. These estimates are not certified RdSAP calculations and cannot be used as evidence of an EPC rating for MEES compliance purposes.

To confirm any EPC rating for regulatory purposes, a new official EPC must be lodged by a qualified Domestic Energy Assessor (DEA) following completion of any improvements.

Nothing on EPC2C constitutes legal advice. The information about MEES regulations, exemptions, and compliance obligations is provided for general information only. Landlords should seek independent legal advice if they are uncertain about their specific obligations.

MEES legislation and government guidance may change. EPC2C attempts to reflect current regulations but cannot guarantee that all information is current at the time of your use.

Estimates are indicative only

Cost estimates, EPC band uplift projections, and compliance pathway suggestions provided by EPC2C are indicative planning-level estimates only. They are based on:

  • Data from the government EPC register (accuracy dependent on the lodged EPC)
  • GoGreen Alliance’s experience with West Midlands installation costs
  • A simplified SAP sequential subtraction methodology

Actual costs and outcomes will vary based on property condition, contractor pricing, material costs, and site-specific factors. EPC2C accepts no liability for any losses arising from reliance on cost or band uplift estimates.

The Verified Compliance Survey (Tier 3) produces higher-confidence estimates based on actual measured property dimensions, but these remain estimates until a lodged EPC confirms the rating.
