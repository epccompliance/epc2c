How it works

No extra site visits.
No new systems to learn.

EPC2C slots into what you already do. The automated Tier 2 tool runs entirely on EPC register data — no additional visit needed. Tier 3 uses your existing RdSAP XML and site notes.

Step 01
Complete your EPC as normal
Your standard EPC assessment. No changes to your existing process or software. Your RdSAP XML is already everything needed for Tier 3.
Step 02
Generate the compliance report
Enter the postcode, select the property. EPC2C pulls the register data, runs the MEES compliance model, and produces a branded PDF within seconds.
Step 03
Deliver to your landlord client
Hand over the compliance plan alongside your EPC certificate. The landlord gets a clear action plan — you earn an additional fee on every assessment.
