Why this matters for agents

Your landlords need guidance.
Most don't know where to start.

MEES compliance is complex. Landlords are confused about costs, exemptions, and deadlines. The agents who step in with clear, actionable advice will be the ones who retain clients through 2030 and beyond.

⚠ What's at stake for your landlord clients

Property unlettableA property below EPC C cannot be legally let or re-let after 2030 without a registered exemption.
