About

Built by the people
who do the work

GoGreen Alliance has been at the forefront of domestic energy assessment and retrofit in the West Midlands since 2016.

As qualified DEAs and retrofit coordinators, we recognised that landlords were receiving EPCs that told them what improvements existed — but not the most practical or economical route to compliance.

EPC2C was built to fill that gap. Developed by practitioners who carry out real assessments on real properties and install improvements, it is the only MEES compliance planning tool built from the inside out — by the people who do the work.

We are the West Midlands’ leading MEES compliance specialists. Our team holds qualifications in domestic energy assessment, retrofit coordination, and has direct experience registering MEES exemptions on behalf of landlords across the region.

Report estimates use the official EPC register’s figures — national indicative banding, not West Midlands-specific. GoGreen Alliance’s own m² rates apply only to the quotations we provide. The aim is to give landlords the information they need to plan confidently — not just a list of what might be possible.
