I want you to perform a deep viability and market assessment of my startup project: EPC2C.co.uk.

The platform is designed to help UK landlords understand the cheapest and most practical route to achieving EPC C compliance ahead of MEES 2030 regulations.

Current stage:

* Early prototype deployed on Vercel
* Uses EPC API data for property lookup
* User searches address and selects property
* Goal is to generate a simplified EPC-to-C compliance pathway report
* Initially testing with landlords and DEA (Domestic Energy Assessor) users
* Planned low-cost validation pricing around £9.99
* Considering freemium or partner-led models later

Core concept:
The platform should eventually allow users to:

* View current EPC details
* Understand likely upgrade measures
* Experiment with upgrade selections
* See estimated EPC improvements and costs
* Understand MEES exemption pathways where relevant
* Potentially connect with installers/funding options

The broader vision is:

* A scalable landlord compliance platform
* B2B tools for letting agents, DEAs and installers
* White-label opportunities
* Portfolio compliance dashboards
* Retrofit lead generation engine
* Potential integration with grants/funding/installer fulfilment

I want an honest, commercial and strategic assessment — not encouragement.

* How large is the potential market?
* What segments are strongest (landlords, agents, DEAs, lenders etc.)?

   Review companies/platforms such as:

* GreenVal

* UX

3. Commercial model assessment
   Evaluate:
